Fraud Warning — Recovery Scams

Beware of "recovery scams" — fraudulent services that target scam victims a second time. FraudFund Recovery:

  • Does NOT charge large upfront fees before evaluating your case
  • Does NOT guarantee financial recovery
  • Does NOT claim special access to your funds
  • Does NOT claim to work directly with banks or cryptocurrency exchanges to release money

If anyone contacts you claiming to be FraudFund Recovery and makes such promises, do not engage and report it to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
