What a Case May Involve

We review many online-fraud and financial-dispute case types. Each one is unique and needs the facts and verifiable evidence.

Romance fraud

Cases involving relationships used to move money or gifts under a false identity.

Investment schemes

Fake funds, guaranteed-return pitches, and unlicensed trading desks.

Exchange and mining disputes

Cloned platforms, platform access difficulties, and withdrawal concerns.

Online task schemes

Paid-task apps and commission platforms where deposits were not returned as expected.

Impersonation fraud

Someone posing as a bank, government office, company, or a trusted person.

Phishing, fake checks, malware, credit-card fraud, and schemes that do not fit a neat label.
