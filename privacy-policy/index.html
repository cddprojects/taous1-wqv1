Last updated: January 1, 2026

FraudFund Recovery LLC ("FraudFund Recovery," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We use essential, analytics (Google Analytics), and advertising cookies (Google Ads conversion tracking). You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with:

We retain your information as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes in this policy. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. No internet transmission is completely secure.
