About FraudFund Recovery

We are a US-based fraud reporting assistance service dedicated to helping scam victims navigate the path to justice — clearly, honestly, and without false promises.

Our Mission

Helping Victims Take Informed Action

Online fraud devastates lives. In 2023 alone, Americans reported losing over $10 billion to scams — and millions more go unreported because victims don't know where to turn or feel too ashamed to speak up.

FraudFund Recovery was founded to bridge that gap. We provide clear, compassionate guidance to help fraud victims understand their options, organize their cases, file official reports with the right agencies, and connect with experienced fraud attorneys when needed.

We believe every victim deserves clear information and a respectful partner — not empty promises or exploitation.
